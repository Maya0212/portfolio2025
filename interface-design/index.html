Overview

Pioneer Saloon is a Japanese plate lunch restaurant. Their original store is located on Monsarrat Avenue, and they are now also operating in Kaka'ak. This rustic casual restaurant is beloved by both locals and visitors alike.

Challenge

They have not updated their website in a long time, therefore, same of the menu has changed and the merchandise page has never been updated. In addition, they have a lot of menu items, so it was crowded and difficult to navigate.

Solution

I made merchandise page and the menu sections are arranged horizintally due to the menu can be easily accessed at a glance.

Rustic casual or Shiny Beach

Pioneer saloon has rustic casual style and displays antiques that are the owner's pick. The original place which is on Monsarrat Ave is nearby Waikiki beach. You can take your plate to neaby beatch and enjoy Japanese&Hawaiian plate lunch. The design reflects their style, resulting in a blend of vintage and Hawaiian chill vibes.
